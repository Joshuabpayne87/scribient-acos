Questions surgeons ask

Straight answers.

How was accuracy measured?

We ran the same visits through our transcript-to-note pipeline in multiple tests, using models from two different providers, and checked the finished notes and orders against what was said. Ask us at booth 105 and we'll walk you through the results.

Who signs off on the chart?

You do. Nothing is final until you sign. You scan the note, confirm the few values Scribient flags, and one signature closes the chart, its orders and its documents.

Do you sign a BAA?

Yes. We sign a Business Associate Agreement with every practice, every signature is tamper-evident, and every read and signature is kept in an audit trail.

What happens to my current templates and system?

A dedicated setup specialist rebuilds your paper or current software templates when you sign up, so your notes look the way you already write them from the first visit. After that, every note is finished at the end of the visit. Tell us what you use today and we'll plan the move with you.
